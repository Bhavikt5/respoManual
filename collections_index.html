Module 01
Who We Are and What We Do
Understanding Respo's mission and how the recovery team fits in.

About Respo Financial Capital

Respo Financial Capital Private Limited is a Reserve Bank of India (RBI) registered Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC). We provide unsecured personal loans to individuals.

Our loans range from 6 to 36 months in tenure and are disbursed entirely digitally with no physical branches.

Our Promise: Respo is committed to providing credit in a fair, transparent, and responsible manner.

Our Regulatory Framework

As an RBI-regulated NBFC, Respo and its employees including Recovery agents must follow a set of rules when dealing with borrowers - especially when it comes to recovering dues.

Regulation What It Covers
RBI RBC-MD 2025 including any amendments thereto Specific rules for recovery, calling hours, Fair treatment of borrowers, prohibited recovery practices, grievance redressal
Respo Fair Practice Code Our commitment to borrowers - published on our website
Respo Code of Conduct (Recovery) Your specific do's and don'ts as a Recovery team member

The Recovery Team's Role at Respo

How you speak to and treat borrowers directly shapes:

  • The borrower's experience with Respo
  • Respo's reputation and RBI compliance
  • Our ability to recover dues sustainably
Remember: A borrower who is treated fairly and with dignity is far more likely to repay than one who feels harassed or threatened. Fair recovery practice is also effective recovery practice.
Module 02
Your Role in Recovery
What you are responsible for and how the recovery process works at Respo.

Key Definitions

Term Meaning
DPD Days Past Due - number of days since the EMI was due but not paid
NPA Non-Performing Asset - a loan classified as bad debt at 90 DPD
EMI Equated Monthly Instalment - the fixed monthly repayment amount
Overdue Interest Interest on unpaid EMI at the contracted rate (not an additional charge)
Late Payment Penalty A capped daily penalty for delayed payment
GRO Grievance Redressal Officer - the person a borrower can formally complain to

Your Core Objectives — Step by Step

  1. Contact the borrower promptly — reach out as soon as an EMI is missed using approved channels and timing.
  2. Understand the borrower's situation — find out why the EMI was missed and when can the borrower make the payment.
  3. Offer a resolution path — payment within the cycle, part payment, or escalation for a formal arrangement.
  4. Document every interaction — log all calls, SMS, and outcomes in the collections system immediately after each interaction.
  5. Escalate when needed — if the borrower is unresponsive or is threatening, escalate to your supervisor.
Your Identity on Every Call: You must always identify yourself by name and as a representative of Respo Financial Capital at the start of every call. Never call from personal numbers — use only Respo-registered contact numbers.
Module 03
Rules of Engagement
The specific rules governing when, how, and who you can contact — RBI-mandated, not optional

Calling Hours

Mandatory Rule: You may ONLY contact borrowers between 8:00 AM and 7:00 PM. Calls, SMS, WhatsApp, or any other contact outside these hours is prohibited — regardless of how urgent the case is.

This rule applies to all forms of contact: phone calls, SMS messages, WhatsApp messages, and any other digital communication.

Who You Can Contact

Critical Rule: You may ONLY contact the borrower. You must NEVER discuss the borrower's loan dues with anyone else — not family members, not employers, not friends. References contact information should only be contacted if the borrower is uncontactable and you are calling the reference seeking help in connecting with the Borrower. No critical information about the loan should be provided to the reference.

Identification on Every Call

At the start of every call, you must state:

  1. Your full name clearly
  2. That you are calling on behalf of Respo Financial Capital Private Limited
  3. The purpose of the call (EMI follow-up)
  4. That the call is being recorded
  5. Confirm you are speaking with the borrower (never assume)
📞 Sample Call Opening
✗ Wrong
(Launching straight into overdue amount and threats without identifying yourself)
✓ Right
"Good morning. Am I speaking with [Borrower Name]? This is [Your Name] calling from Respo Financial Capital. I am calling regarding your loan account. Please note this call is being recorded"

Using Only Registered Numbers

All borrower contacts must be made only from Respo-registered phone numbers. Using your personal mobile number to contact a borrower is a violation of Respo's Code of Conduct, a potential violation of the borrower's privacy rights, and grounds for disciplinary action.

Number of Contacts — Avoid Excessive Calling

The following practices are strictly prohibited:

  • Back-to-back call attempts to the same borrower. A minimum interval of 15 minutes must be maintained between successive call attempts.
  • Excessive SMS or WhatsApp communication. A minimum interval of 30 mins must be maintained between successive SMS or WhatsApp reminders to the same borrower.
  • Repeated calls or messages that are likely to cause inconvenience, annoyance, intimidation, or undue pressure on the borrower.
Module 04
What You Must Never Do
Prohibited practices under RBI guidelines and Respo's Code of Conduct — zero tolerance
Zero Tolerance Policy: Respo has a zero tolerance approach to unethical recovery practices. Any violation of the rules in this module will be investigated and may result in termination of your contract or employment.

Absolutely Prohibited — No Exceptions

Prohibited Practice
Using abusive, threatening, or obscene language
Threatening physical harm or any other harm
Calling outside 8 AM – 7 PM
Making anonymous or disguised calls
Contacting employer, family, or friends about the loan
Posting borrower details on social media or any public platform
Making false statements about legal consequences
Impersonating a court official, police officer, or government authority
Collecting cash from the borrower personally
Excessive or repeated calling to harass

✓ WHAT YOU SHOULD SAY

  • "I understand your situation. Can we discuss a way to resolve this?"
  • "Your payment of ₹X is overdue. Would you like to make a partial payment today?"
  • "If you are facing financial difficulty, please speak to our team - we may be able to help."
  • "Your account will be reported to the credit bureau if the payment is not received by [date]."

✗ WHAT YOU MUST NEVER SAY

  • "I will send someone to your house" (unless a formal visit has been notified and approved)
  • "I will tell your employer about this"
  • "You will go to jail if you don't pay"
  • "Pay now or else..." (threats of any kind)
  • Any abusive or caste/religion-based language

Social Media — Complete Prohibition

You must never post a borrower's name, photo, contact details, or loan information on any social media platform. You must never share borrower information in WhatsApp groups, Telegram channels, or any public forum.

No Cash Collections

Respo is a fully digital lender. We do not accept cash. If a borrower offers cash payment:

  1. Do not accept it under any circumstances.
  2. Guide the borrower to pay through the Zype app or send them Respo’s authorized payment link from Payment Gateway.
Module 05
Handling Borrower Calls
Practical guidance on how to structure effective, compliant borrower conversations.

The 5-Step Call Framework

Step Action Description
1 Identify yourself and confirm borrower identity. State your name, Respo Financial Capital, and confirm you are speaking with the borrower. Mention the call is recorded.
2 State the purpose clearly and respectfully. Mention the overdue amount and the number of days past due. Keep it factual - no threats, no drama.
3 Listen actively. Ask the borrower to explain their situation. Be patient. Do not interrupt.
4 Offer a resolution. Based on what the borrower tells you, offer appropriate next steps. If you are not authorised to offer a specific arrangement, escalate - do not promise something you cannot deliver.
5 Confirm and close. Summarise what was agreed, give the borrower a clear next step, and thank them for speaking with you. Log the call immediately.

Real Call Scenarios

"Borrower says I will pay next week"

📞 Scenario A — Borrower promises to pay next week
✗ Wrong:
"You said that last time too. I am going to report this and take action against you."
✓ Right:
"Since you have missed the payments, please confirm when do you plan to make the payment? I will note this on your account. Please also be aware that additional late charges will apply for each day the payment remains overdue and the reporting of overdue will also be done to credit bureaus"

"Borrower says I lost my job and cannot pay"

📞 Scenario B — Borrower reports financial hardship
✗ Wrong:
"That is not our problem. You took the loan and you need to pay."
✓ Right:
"I am sorry to hear that. I understand this must be a difficult time. Let me note your situation on the account and in the meantime, if you can make even a partial payment today, it will reduce the penalties accumulating on your account. Would that be possible?"

"Borrower becomes abusive on the call"

📞 Scenario C — Borrower is aggressive
✗ Wrong:
Responding with equal aggression or making counter-threats.
✓ Right:
"I understand you are upset. I want to help resolve this for you, but I need us to have a professional conversation."

"Someone other than the borrower answers"

📞 Scenario D — Third party answers the phone
✗ Wrong:
"Please tell [Borrower Name] that they owe ₹X and we will take action."
✓ Right:
"I am looking to speak with [Borrower Name]. Could you please let them know that someone from Respo Financial Capital called and ask them to call back at [Respo number]? Thank you."

After Every Call - What to Log

Immediately after every call, log the following in the collections system:

  • Date and time of call
  • Who answered (borrower / third party / no answer)
  • Summary of the conversation
  • Any commitment made by the borrower (payment date/amount)
  • Next action and date
Module 06
Special Situations
How to handle situations that require extra care and specific procedures.

Borrower Claims Financial Hardship

  1. Listen carefully and do not disrespect their situation.
  2. Acknowledge: "I understand this is a difficult time."
  3. Do NOT promise any specific restructuring, waiver, or arrangement — you are not authorised. Escalate to your supervisor.
  4. Log the call with "Hardship — escalate for review" and notify your supervisor immediately.

Borrower Disputes the Loan or Amount

  1. Do not argue. Take note of the dispute.
  2. Tell the borrower: "I understand you are disputing this. I will flag this on your account and our team will review it."
  3. Log the dispute clearly and escalate to your supervisor immediately.

Borrower Mentions Legal Action or Police Complaint

If a borrower says they will approach a consumer forum, police, or the RBI Ombudsman:

  • Do not panic or become defensive.
  • Respond calmly: "You are absolutely entitled to approach any authority. Our Grievance Redressal Officer can also help resolve your concern, share the contact if needed"
  • Log the call and inform your supervisor. Do not make any threats in response.

Borrower Requests No-Contact

  1. Note the request on the account.
  2. Inform the borrower that the account remains active and dues continue to accrue.
  3. Escalate to your supervisor — do not unilaterally stop all collections activity without authorisation.
  4. Your supervisor will decide the appropriate next step.

Deceased Borrower

  • Speak to your supervisor immediately.
  • Communications with the legal heir (if any) must follow a specific process.
  • Exercise empathy and professionalism at all times when interacting with the family.
Module 07
Grievances and Escalations
Understanding how to handle borrower complaints and when to escalate.

Every Borrower Has the Right to Complain

Under Respo's Fair Practice Code and RBI guidelines, every borrower has the right to raise a grievance. You must never discourage, dismiss, or obstruct a borrower from raising a complaint.

Respo's Grievance Redressal Structure: Level 1 — Customer Service Team: First point of contact. Must respond within 5 working days. Level 2 — Grievance Redressal Officer (GRO): Escalation if Level 1 is unsatisfactory. Must respond within 15 working days. Level 3 — RBI Ombudsman: If unresolved within 30 days or borrower is unsatisfied with Respo's resolution.

When a Borrower Raises a Complaint During a Collections Call

  1. Listen fully to the complaint.
  2. Acknowledge the complain to the borrower on the call.
  3. Provide the GRO contact details — available on the Respo website.
  4. Log the complaint with full details.
  5. Inform your supervisor immediately.

Complaints About You Specifically

  • Do not be defensive or dismissive.
  • Do not contact the borrower again on the same complaint without supervisor authorisation.
  • Report the complaint to your supervisor immediately.
  • All calls are recorded — the recording will be reviewed as part of the grievance process.

RBI Ombudsman

If a borrower tells you they are going to approach the RBI Ombudsman:

  • Acknowledge their right to do so.
  • Inform your supervisor immediately
Module 08
Data and Privacy Rules
Your obligations under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and Respo's data policy.

What Is Borrower Data?

Every piece of information about a borrower you have access to — their name, phone number, address, employer, loan amount, repayment history, bank account — is personal data protected by law.

What You Must Never Do With Borrower Data

  • Share a borrower's name, address, loan details, or any other information with anyone other than the borrower
  • Save borrower data on your personal phone or personal email
  • Screenshot or photograph collections system data on your personal device
  • Discuss borrower information with colleagues unnecessarily
  • Share borrower data on social media, WhatsApp groups, or any external platform

If You Suspect a Data Breach

  1. Do not try to fix it yourself or cover it up.
  2. Report it to your supervisor and the Compliance team immediately.

Using the Collections System Responsibly

  • Log out of the collections system when you are not actively using it
  • Never share your login credentials with anyone
  • Do not access accounts that are not assigned to you
  • Report any system errors or unusual access you notice to your supervisor
