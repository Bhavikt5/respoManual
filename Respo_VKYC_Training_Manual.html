Module 01
What is Video KYC?
Understanding the purpose, regulatory framework, and why Video KYC matters at Respo

What is V-CIP?

Video-based Customer Identification Process (V-CIP) is an alternate method of customer identification where a specially trained official of Respo Financial Capital conducts a live, real-time audio-visual interaction with the customer to:

  • Verify the customer's identity using their face, documents, and live responses
  • Complete Customer Due Diligence (CDD) as required by the RBI KYC Directions 2025
  • Obtain the customer's informed consent for the process
  • Maintain a complete audit trail of the interaction

Why Does Respo Use V-CIP?

Respo is a fully digital lender — customers apply for loans using digital channels. V-CIP allows Respo to onboard eligible customers in a compliant, secure, and fully digital manner in line wih Respo’s KYC policy.

What V-CIP Achieves

What V-CIP Achieves How
Identity verification Live face matching with PAN photograph
Liveness confirmation Real-time interaction — varied questions, live GPS, date-time stamp
Document verification PAN display
Consent recording Customer consents on camera before session begins
Fraud prevention Face liveness/spoof detection, IP verification, concurrent audit
Module 02
Your Role as a V-CIP Official
What you are responsible for and the standards you must meet

Your Core Responsibilities

As a V-CIP official at Respo, you are responsible for:

  1. Conducting live, real-time V-CIP sessions with customers through the V-CIP platform
  2. Verifying customer identity by matching face and PAN
  3. Performing liveness checks by asking varied, real-time questions — not scripted responses
  4. Detecting fraudulent manipulation, prompting, pre-recorded videos, or suspicious conduct
  5. Capturing all required data — photographs, documents, address, financial information
  6. Maintaining your activity log and credential record for every session conducted
  7. Escalating suspicious cases to your supervisor immediately

What You Need Before You Start Any Session

  • Your Respo-assigned V-CIP system login — never share credentials with anyone
  • A stable internet connection from Respo's secured network domain
  • The V-CIP SOP (Standard Operating Procedure) — know it thoroughly before your first session
  • Access to the negative/watchlist in the system — check customer name before proceeding

Obtaining Informed Consent — Mandatory Before Anything Else

Before asking any verification question, you must obtain the customer's informed consent on camera. This is a mandatory regulatory requirement.

📋 How to Obtain Consent on Camera
✗ Wrong: Starting the verification immediately without informing the customer of the purpose or obtaining consent.
✓ Right: "Good [morning/afternoon]. My name is [Your Name] and I am an authorised official of Respo Financial Capital Private Limited. I am conducting a Video KYC verification for your loan application. This session will be recorded. Do you consent to this Video KYC process being conducted and recorded today? Please say Yes if you agree.”

Module 04
Conducting the V-CIP Session
Step-by-step guide to running a compliant, accurate V-CIP session

The V-CIP Session — Step by Step

  1. Obtain informed consent on camera (see Module 3). Do not proceed without it.
  2. Confirm customer identity — ask the customer to state their full name and date of birth. Cross-check against the details in the system.
  3. Perform liveness check — ask VARIED questions (do not use the same sequence as the previous session)
  4. Document verification — instruct the customer to display PAN on camera. Verify that the photograph on the document matches the customer on screen. Verify all details.
  5. PAN verification — capture a clear image of the PAN card.
  6. Economic/financial profile — confirm the customer's financial and economic information as submitted in the loan application during the session.
  7. Close the session —inform the customer the session is complete, and that the account will be activated after concurrent audit.
  8. Log everything — update the activity log with session outcome, documents verified, any exceptions, and your credentials immediately after the session.

Handling Disruptions During the Session

If the call drops or disconnects:
  1. Abort the current session.
  2. Initiate a completely fresh session from the beginning.
  3. Obtain consent again at the start of the new session.
  4. Log the dropped session with the reason for discontinuation.

Module 06
What to Reject and Why
Clear criteria for rejecting a V-CIP session — fraud indicators and red flags
Your Guiding Principle: When in doubt, reject the session and escalate to your supervisor. It is better to reject a genuine customer (who can reapply) than to onboard a fraudulent customer. Your judgment and the concurrent audit are the final safeguards.

Mandatory Rejection Criteria

You MUST reject a V-CIP session and must NOT complete onboarding in the following situations:

Rejection Criterion What to Do
Customer does not give clear verbal consent End session; log as "No consent obtained"
Photograph on PAN does not match the customer on screen Reject; log the mismatch; escalate to supervisor
Prompting detected — someone coaching the customer off-screen Reject; log "Prompting detected"; escalate
Liveness check failed — pre-recorded video, photograph, or deepfake suspected Reject immediately; log; escalate to supervisor and IT security team
Customer refuses to display PAN card on camera Reject; log reason
Printed copy of e-PAN or equivalent e-document presented Ask for original or digital version; if unavailable, reject
Session was previously rejected for fraud — same customer attempting again Check system flag before proceeding; escalate to supervisor before any session
Any other fraud suspected Rejected and escalate to supervisor

Fraud Indicators — Stay Alert For These

📋 Scenario A — Suspected Pre-Recorded Video
✗ Wrong: Continuing the session because the face looks real and the document matches.
✓ Right: Abort the session. Ask the customer to perform a spontaneous task (hold up fingers, turn sideways). If they cannot respond naturally and immediately, reject and log "Liveness failure — pre-recorded video suspected." Escalate to supervisor and IT security team. This is a cyber fraud attempt and must be reported.
📋 Scenario B — Third Party Coaching the Customer
✗ Wrong: Continuing because the customer eventually answers the questions (with help).
✓ Right: Reject the session immediately. Log "Prompting detected — third party visible/audible during session." Escalate to supervisor. A genuine customer undergoes V-CIP independently. Any coaching is a red flag for identity fraud.
📋 Scenario C — Documents Match But Face Does Not
✗ Wrong: Accepting the session because the documents look genuine.
✓ Right: Reject the session. Log "Facial mismatch — photograph on document does not match customer on screen." This is a strong indicator of identity fraud — someone is attempting to pass off as another person using genuine documents.

After Rejection — What to Do

  1. Log the session as rejected with clear, specific reasons.
  2. Inform your supervisor immediately — particularly for cases involving suspected fraud, or liveness failures.
  3. For detected cases of forged identity, report it as a cyber event under Respo's IT security reporting process.
  4. Do NOT inform the customer of the specific fraud indicators detected — avoid tipping them off.
  5. The rejected session record must be preserved — it will be considered if the customer reapplies.
Module 07
After the Session
Concurrent audit, data storage, and record keeping obligations

Concurrent Audit — Mandatory Before Account Activation

Non-Negotiable Requirement: ALL accounts opened through V-CIP must be subject to concurrent audit BEFORE the account is made operational. You do not activate the account — the concurrent audit team does. Never skip or bypass this step.

The concurrent audit serves to:

  • Verify the integrity of the V-CIP process you conducted
  • Confirm that all required steps were completed correctly
  • Provide an independent check on your identification determination
  • Ensure the overall acceptability of the outcome before the customer's account goes live

After completing a session, submit it for concurrent audit through the system. The audit team will review the recording, documents captured, and your activity log. If the audit raises any query, respond promptly with complete information.

Data Storage

Rule Detail
Storage location All data and recordings must be stored in systems located in India only
Cloud data ownership If cloud is used, all data must transfer to Respo's exclusively owned/leased server immediately after V-CIP is complete. No data retained by cloud service provider or third-party tech provider.
Time and date stamp All recordings must bear time and date stamp for easy historical search
Encryption End-to-end encryption must be maintained between customer device and the hosting point at all times
Retention period Records must be maintained for at least 5 years from the date of the session or end of the business relationship, whichever is later
Module 08
Data Privacy and Confidentiality
Your obligations under the DPDP Act 2023, Aadhaar regulations, and Respo's data policy

Customer Data is Protected by Law

Every piece of customer information you access during a V-CIP session — Aadhaar number, PAN, photograph, address, financial details, the video recording itself — is personal data protected by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023.

Strict Rule: You may only use customer data collected during V-CIP for one purpose: to complete the KYC verification process on behalf of Respo Financial Capital. Any other use of this data is a criminal offence under the DPDP Act 2023.

What You Must Never Do With Customer Data

  • Save any customer data (photo, Aadhaar, PAN, video screenshot) on your personal device
  • Share customer information with colleagues who are not directly involved in the V-CIP process for that customer
  • Discuss customer identity details in open office spaces where others can hear
  • Take screenshots or screen recordings of the V-CIP session on your personal device
  • Share any customer information outside Respo's systems via email, WhatsApp, or any other channel
